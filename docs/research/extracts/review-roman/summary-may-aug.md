## Missing Festivals (May–Aug)

- [Maius] Day 1: Maia — sacrifice of pregnant sow by Flamen Volcanalis; month name may derive from her (HIGH)
- [Maius] Day 11: Mania — Fasti Antiquitatis Maioris records sacrifice to Mania (Mater Larum) on same day as Lemuria (LOW)
- [Maius] Day 14: Mars Invictus — temple anniversary in Circus Flaminius (pridie Idus); Fasti Venusini (MEDIUM)
- [Maius] Day 23: Feriae Volcano — separately attested in Fasti Venusini + Amiterninum; app collapses it into the Tubilustrium (MEDIUM)
- [Iunius] Day 1 (a): Mars in Clivo — dies natalis of temple of Mars extra portam Capenam (c. 388 BC) (LOW)
- [Iunius] Day 1 (b): Tempestates — temple vowed by L. Cornelius Scipio after storm off Corsica (LOW)
- [Iunius] Day 7: Vesta Aperitur — opening of Vestal penus; Philocalus calendar ("Vesta aperit"); religious period runs 7–15 June (MEDIUM)
- [Iunius] Day 11: Fortuna Virgo in Foro Boario — dedicated same day as Mater Matuta by Servius Tullius; Scullard pp.151–152 (MEDIUM)
- [Iunius] Days 25–26: Ludi Taurei — quinquennial games in Circus Flaminius for Di inferi; keys "5-25" and "5-26" have no entry (MEDIUM)
- [Iunius] Day 27: Lares V[...] — attested Ant. mai.; interpretation uncertain (LOW)
- [Iulius] Day 6: Fortunae Muliebri — temple dedication on Via Latina; cult restricted to univirae; Scullard pp.160–161 (MEDIUM)
- [Iulius] Day 7 (a): Consus in Circo — sacrifice at underground Consus altar (first meta of Circus Maximus) (MEDIUM)
- [Iulius] Day 8: Vitulatio — sacrifice to Vitula (goddess of victory/joyful song) with pontifical attendance; attested in both sources (HIGH)
- [Iulius] Day 17: Victoriae in Capitolio — separate C-status sacrifice to Victoria; gold statue from Hiero of Syracuse (MEDIUM)
- [Iulius] Days 20–30: Ludi Victoriae Caesaris — eleven-day games vowed at Pharsalus; keys "6-20"–"6-29" currently blank C-days (MEDIUM)
- [Augustus] Day 1: Victoria / Victoria Virgo — two Palatine temple dedications (294 BC, 193 BC); entirely absent (MEDIUM)
- [Augustus] Day 12: Hercules Invictus at Ara Maxima + Venus Victrix at Pompey's Theatre — linked to WRONG_DATE finding on day 11 (HIGH)
- [Augustus] Day 13: Vortumnus, Fortuna Equestris, Hercules Victor, Castor & Pollux in Circo, Camenae, Flora — five additional cult entries absent (MEDIUM)
- [Augustus] Day 17: Janus ad theatrum Marcelli (LOW)
- [Augustus] Day 19: Venus Libitina — temple in Lucus Libitinae on Esquiline; headquarters of libitinarii and death registrations (MEDIUM)
- [Augustus] Day 24: Luna in Graecostasi — Fasti Pinciani (LOW)

## Invented Aitia (May–Aug)

- [Iulius] Day 19: Lucaria — app states "donde los romanos se ocultaron tras el desastre del Alia"; both Scullard (pp.166–167) and Fowler (pp.182–185) explicitly call this a secondary aetiological invention formed after the ritual's meaning was lost; genuine purpose was propitiation of woodland spirits before tree-felling

## Minor Findings (May–Aug)

- [Maius] Day 12: Ludi Martiales — app states temple of Mars Ultor dedicated this day, but Res Gestae 21.2 gives 1 August (MEDIUM)
- [Maius] Day 15: Maia omitted from deity — ancient calendars name Maia alongside Mercury and Jupiter for the Ides of May (LOW)
- [Iunius] Day 3: Bellona — omits columella (pillar = symbolic enemy territory) and fetial spear-casting rite for declaring war on overseas enemies (LOW)
- [Iunius] Day 4: Hercules Magnus Custos — deity listed as "Hercules Custos"; both sources give full epithet (LOW)
- [Iunius] Day 11: Matralia — description omits (1) univira restriction, (2) slave-girl beaten and expelled, (3) testuacia cakes (LOW)
- [Iunius] Day 24: Fors Fortuna — listed as generic "Fortuna"; both sources distinguish "Fors Fortuna" (trans Tiberim) as a separate cult (LOW)
- [Iunius] Day 30: Hercules Musarum — description omits nine Muses' statues from Ambracia and Hercules lyre detail (LOW)
- [Iulius] Days 19 & 21: Lucaria status — Scullard: NP; Fowler: IP; sources disagree, no change required (LOW)
- [Iulius] Day 22: Concordia — "otra fundación" implies subordinacy; this is the primary July dedication (LOW)
- [Iulius] Day 23: Neptunalia — Salacia (Neptune's consort) not listed; single source (Fowler) (LOW)
- [Augustus] Day 3: Supplicia Canum — describes only dog punishment; omits equally attested goose procession (LOW)
- [Augustus] Day 5: Salus — description says "altar de la salud"; both sources describe a temple (LOW)
