## Missing Festivals (Sep–Dec)

- September Day 5: Dedicatio Iovis Statoris — dies natalis of Q. Caecilius Metellus Macedonicus's temple of Jupiter Stator (146 BC); entirely absent from "8-5" (subsumed by Ludi Romani entry)
- September Days 20–23: Mercatus — post-Ludi market days following the Ludi Romani; "8-20" through "8-23" have no Mercatus entry
- October Day 14: Penatibus in Velia — named calendar entry (EN) for the Temple of the Penates Dei on the Velia; "9-14" has correct status EN but no festivalName or festivalDesc
- October Day 15: Ludi Capitolini — private games by the College of Capitolini; attested by Cicero, Plutarch, Tertullian; not mentioned in "9-15"
- November Day 13: Feronia in Campo — "Feroniae in Campo" on the Ides of November; freedwomen contributed offerings; absent from "10-13" and incorrectly placed on day 15
- November Day 13: Fortuna Primigenia — dies natalis of her Capitol temple (dedicated 194 BC; Livy 29.36, 34.53); absent from "10-13"
- November Day 13: [Pietati] — dies natalis of the temple of Pietas in the Forum Holitorium; partially preserved in Antiate calendar; absent from "10-13"
- December Day 1: Neptuno et Pietati — dies natales of the temple of Neptune and temple of Pietas; absent from "11-1"
- December Day 10: Tribuni Plebis — tribunes of the plebs assumed office on 10 December (Praenestine Fasti); absent from "11-10"
- December Day 12: Consualia I (Aventino) — dies natalis of the temple of Consus on the Aventine (status EN); "11-12" has correct EN status but no festivalName, deity, or description
- December Day 21: Sacrifice to Hercules and Ceres — sacrifice of a pregnant sow with loaves and mulsum; absent from "11-21" (Scullard p.210)
- December Day 23: Dianae et Iunoni Reginae — dies natales of Diana's temple and Juno Regina's temple in Campo, both dedicated 179 BC by M. Aemilius Lepidus; absent from "11-23"

## Minor Findings (Sep–Dec)

- September Day 23: Dedicatio Apollini et Latonae — festivalDesc attributes the 431 BC original dedication to Sep 23; what falls on Sep 23 is the Sosian restoration (c. 32 BC); the original 431 BC date is uncertain (possibly July 13)
- September Day 26: Dedicatio Veneri Genetrici — festivalDesc omits the Pharsalus vow aition (Caesar's vow before Pharsalus 48 BC), which Scullard describes as the explicit founding occasion
- October Day 5: Mundus Patet — deity "Augusto / Manes" conflates two distinct rites (Ludi Augustales and Mundus Patet)
- October Day 19: Armilustrium — festivalDesc says "armas de las legiones eran purificadas y devueltas a depósito"; the Armilustrium was a Salian ceremony using the ancilia (sacred shields at the Regia), not a legionary logistics operation
- October Day 31: Ludi Victoriae Sullanae — festivalDesc says "Último día natural del mes" implying games end Oct 31; games continued into and culminated on 1 November
- November Day 1: festivalName "Ludi Sullanae" is an incomplete truncation; full attested name is "Ludi Victoriae Sullanae"
- December Day 22: Lares Permarini — festivalName shows "Saturnalia," obscuring the distinct dies natalis of the Lares Permarini temple; festivalDesc says "altar" but the structure is a temple (aedes)
